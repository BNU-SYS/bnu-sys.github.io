At the national finals of the **2026 National College Student Computer System Capability Competition – Intelligent Computing Innovation Design Contest (XianDaoBei)**, the BNU-SYS "PiedPiper" team won the **Third Prize**. Jianhua Gao and Jianjun Shi received the **Outstanding Instructor Award**.

## About the Competition

The XianDaoBei contest is organized by the National Association of Computer Education for Higher Education Institutions and the Organizing Committee of the National College Student Computer System Capability Competition, aiming to advance talent development in computer system capability and intelligent computing. This edition featured several tasks; one of them, "Optimization of Qwen Large-Model Inference Services on Domestic Accelerators", required teams to carry out system-level optimization of large-model inference services on domestic accelerator platforms.

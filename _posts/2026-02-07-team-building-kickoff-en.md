To systematically review the group's annual progress and further align its development goals and collaboration rhythm, BNU-SYS held its "2025 Year-End Review" on February 9, 2026. Faculty, postdoctoral researchers, and graduate students took part, presenting and exchanging views under the theme "Looking Back at 2025, Looking Ahead to 2026," with in-depth discussion and a pragmatic atmosphere.

## Event Overview

- **Date:** February 9, 2026
- **Venue:** [Location]
- **Participants:** 17 in total — faculty, postdoctoral researchers, doctoral and master's students
- **Theme:** Looking Back at 2025, Looking Ahead to 2026

The event centered on three main threads — "annual review, problem identification, and goal planning" — emphasizing a results-oriented approach to advancing research collaboration and continuously improving the group's overall execution and capacity for innovative output.

## Main Sessions

1. **Review of 2025 Work**  
   A systematic summary of research progress, papers and project outcomes, academic exchange, and team building, mapping out the milestones reached in each research direction.

2. **Sharing of Representative Results and Experience**  
   Faculty and senior students shared practical experience from the year's key work — including research topic selection, experiment execution, paper writing, and cross-direction collaboration — distilling reusable methods and workflows.

3. **Issue Review and Improvement Suggestions**  
   Participants discussed topics such as "efficiency of cross-direction collaboration," "knowledge-sharing mechanisms," and "task-rhythm management," identifying current bottlenecks and forming improvement suggestions.

4. **Goal Planning for 2026**  
   Aligned with the group's overall direction, the session defined the year's work plans for key research themes, phased task breakdown, optimization of collaboration mechanisms, and talent development.

## Significance

The year-end review not only completed a systematic recap of the 2025 work but also further consolidated team consensus and clarified the key tasks and roadmaps for 2026. Going forward, BNU-SYS will continue to refine its dual-driven mechanism of "academic training + team collaboration," fostering a rigorous, open, mutually supportive, and sustainable research ecosystem.
